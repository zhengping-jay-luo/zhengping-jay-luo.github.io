Do One Cool Thing (DOCT) Program


Back to Homepage

Goals

To provide students with an opportunity to develop their own professional personality, practice public speaking and presentation skills, and to encourage creative thinking and experiential learning.

Assignment Description

For this assignment, each student will give a 5–10 minute individual presentation on one cool activity they completed this semester that is related to this course. You may choose to present:

  • A state-of-the-art technology topic related to the course that you are passionate about
  • A program or project you developed to solve an interesting problem
  • Any other relevant work or experience connected to the course content
  • Your presentation topic must be explicitly related to a concept or topic discussed in this class.
  • You may deliver your presentation using slides, a poster, a live demonstration, or another format of your choice. Presentations may be scheduled during any regular class meeting prior to the final presentation week.
  • This is an individual assignment. Additional details and expectations will be discussed in class.
