About Dr. Jay

Dr. Jay is a dedicated computer science and cybersecurity researcher and educator. He earned his Ph.D. in Systems and Security from the University of South Florida. He is also a Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP), credentialed by the International Information System Security Certification Consortium (ISC)². While not working, he loves traveling, exploring art, and thinking. Some of the professional services and roles he has been involved include:

  • Committee Member, the 41st CCSC Eastern Regional Conference 2025
  • Guest Editor of MDPI Special Issue "Network and Information Security", 2025
  • Advisor, ACM Club (since 2022), Asian American Students Association (since 2025), Rider University
  • NSF Panelist (IUSE, CyberTraining, EDU), 2024, 2025
  • Reviewer, Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC), 2026
  • Reviewer, American Society for Engineering Education (ASEE), 2026
  • Reviewer, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, 2025
  • Reviewer, IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking (ToN), 2024

You can reach him through his email: zluo@rider.edu and LinkedIn.
