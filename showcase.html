AISec Student Research Showcase

Student Poster Presentations and Awards

  • Scope: This is a showcase for student research projects in AI Security and Privacy, AI algorithms, LLMs, AI applications and related projects. Other research projects are also welcomed but will not be considered for awards.
  • Time: 10:00-12:00PM, December 15, 2026.
  • Location: First-Floor Hallway, Mike and Patti Hennessy Science and Technology Center
  • Awards: First place: $400 (1 award), Second place: $300 (2 awards), Third place: $100 (4 awards).
  • Eligibility: All Rider undergraduate students.
  • Submission: Please submit your poster in PDF format to zluo@rider.edu. The submission subject should be "AISec Student Research Showcase - [Your Name]". The poster dimensions should be 48 inches by 36 inches. We will print out the poster for you.
  • Submission Deadline: December 6 23:59PM, 2026.
  • Judging: The posters will be judged by a panel of external experts.
